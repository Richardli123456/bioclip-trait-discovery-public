Visible-trait follow-up protocol recommendation
Prepared 2026-10-04, before new follow-up images are generated.
Scope: planning and CPU-only inspection; this file records no training or image-generation result.

DECISION GATE
Run the generator-aligned SAE seed-stability experiment first. Use its locked matching rule and thresholds, and require a qualifying counterpart in every new SAE run before calling a coordinate reproducible. Match by activation behavior AND decoder direction, not numerical feature ID. A recurrent family association alone is insufficient. Preserve and report all failed matches, not only selected successes.
If neither original lead (ladybird 729, owl/potoo 1969) passes the gate, do not launch hundreds of feature-specific images or rename a different feature after inspecting outputs. Save the negative stability result and this prospective protocol. A new representation/training design is then a separate experiment. A stable coordinate is eligible for testing, not established as a biological trait.
If one lead passes, proceed only with that lead. Do not pool independently trained SAE outputs as if they were extra independent animals.

BIOLOGICAL SOURCE FACTS VERSUS DESIGN CHOICES
Virginia Tech's entomology account describes seven elytral spots for C. septempunctata, including one shared basal spot, and six spots per elytron for C. maculata. Do not include pronotal marks in wing-cover counts. Source: https://virginiafruit.ento.vt.edu/lady.html
The UK Ladybird Survey account describes C. undecimpunctata as having 7-11 spots, with uncommon fusions. Thus eleven is not a universal truth target. Source: https://coleoptera.org.uk/species/coccinella-undecimpunctata
USDA original research reports inherited merging of dark spots in a C. maculata laboratory strain. A generated merged patch should not be 'corrected' to an expected canonical count. Source: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=319616
The UK Ladybird Survey describes A. bipunctata as variable in background color and spot number, including red forms with black spots and black forms with red spots. Source: https://coleoptera.org.uk/node/12913
Potoo upper-eyelid anatomy has been studied: Borrero (1974), Notes on the Structure of the Upper Eyelid of Potoos (Nyctibius), Condor 76:210-211. The publisher/archive landing page was accessible; the former SORA PDF URL redirected to an archive index, so fine anatomical details were not verified from the full paper in this pass. Source: https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/condor/vol76/iss2/16/
The proposed visible-count endpoint, thresholds, seed budget, crop rules, statistical contrasts and stability gate are experiment-design choices. They are not claims taken from these biological sources. No causal relationship between either SAE coordinate and spots or eyes is assumed.

IMAGE GENERATION IF THE STABILITY GATE PASSES
Lock a manifest before generation: source hashes, chosen coordinate and matched coordinates, image seeds, random-control seeds/directions, prompts, dimensions, scheduler, edit strengths and all scoring rules. Use 32 NEW image seeds per condition, distinct from prior seeds42-45. A reproducible proposed list is1000-1031; reuse each seed across all conditions for a species. The old images are pilot data, not part of the new confirmatory count sample.
Use frozen BioCLIP and TaxaAdapter, the saved generator settings (512x512,50 DDIM steps,guidance6,adapter1), no fitting of either model. Intervention is x'=x+D(z')-D(z), retaining the reconstruction residual. Do not re-TopK the edited vector. Original embedding is the true zero-change reference.
Seven minimum conditions: original, unedited reconstruction, selected feature0x,0.5x,2x, removal of a different active feature, and one random-direction edit of exactly the selected-removal input norm, orthogonal to the selected decoder direction. Record the active-control norm mismatch; do not call it biologically unrelated. Random direction must be chosen numerically before viewing outputs. One fixed random direction per species supports a control comparison for that direction only; broader claims about generic random edits require additional prespecified directions.
Use the same prompt in every condition. For a prospective visibility-focused ladybird experiment, suggested prompt: 'best quality, high quality, macro photograph of a single adult beetle, dorsal view, centered, whole body visible, sharp focus'. Do not put species identity, spot count or spot color into the text prompt. This differs from the old generic-prompt pilot, so report the context change and do not combine its effects with pilot effects. If the generic prompt is retained, retain all failed visibility cases; do not regenerate selectively until a pleasing dorsal view appears.
Primary C. septempunctata and both replication species require672 images total at32seeds x7conditions x3species. The replication species are predefined, and counts need not equal their common-name numbers. If only the primary species has a qualifying stable active counterpart, generate only its224 images and report the missing replication.
For cross-SAE replication, repeat only the prespecified key contrasts (original, feature0x,2x, random control) on matched stable coordinates from two additional SAE runs, chosen by fixed seed order rather than favorable output. Treat this as a separate robustness block, not more samples for the original SAE effect.

BLIND ANNOTATION AND CROPS
Assign each image a random opaque ID and random presentation order. Keep condition, SAE seed, generator seed, coordinate, filenames and species names outside the annotator package. Show one image at a time, not condition comparison grids. A person can infer some species visually, but must not see intervention metadata.
Score all224 images per species, including malformed and unscorable outputs. First identify the largest adult beetle by visible body bounding-box area; ties choose the animal whose centroid is nearest the image center, then uppermost/leftmost. Record number of adult-like animals. The primary endpoint requires a single unambiguous adult-like beetle; multiple animals are unscorable for the primary endpoint, while a largest-animal secondary endpoint can be recorded.
Create a tight full-animal axis-aligned bounding box including visible appendages, add5% padding on each side, clip to the image edge, preserve aspect ratio and display without generative resizing. Record native bounding-box coordinates and native body length. Cropping does not recover hidden detail or establish foreground segmentation. Also mark a separate elytra region or polygon for counting. Count in the original pixels/crop; never hallucinate detail from an upscaled view.
Predefined primary count eligibility: recognizable adult beetle; both closed wing covers visible in a dorsal or mildly oblique dorsal view; at least approximately80% of the dorsal elytral surface visibly exposed; neither wing cover substantially truncated; native elytral long axis at least80pixels; focus sufficient to separate patches; no gross anatomical duplication preventing identification of the elytral pair. Record individual eligibility flags so the80% judgment is auditable. Record pose category(dorsal/mild-oblique/lateral/ventral/other), occlusion, crop truncation, open wings, blur, body size and anatomical ambiguity for every image.
Primary measured variable: number of distinct visible dark patches ON the exposed elytra, against a clearly lighter chromatic wing-cover background. Count each contiguous, visually separable patch once. A patch spanning the suture is one if visually continuous; two separated patches are two. Exclude the dark suture itself, outer boundary outline, head, pronotum, legs, shadows, glare and background. Fused spots count as one visible patch; record a fusion flag. Do not split them using expected biological counts. Do not infer hidden spots. Count0 only for a scorable clearly visible wing-cover pair with no dark patches.
Record light-on-dark or indeterminate backgrounds as morphology categories. Dark-on-light count is NA for these images, not0. They may be evidence of a color-form change and must remain in denominators. Optionally record a separate opposite-polarity count, but do not silently combine it with the primary count. For uncertain patch separation, record a lower and upper count; primary exact-count analysis uses only exact confident counts and reports uncertainty rates. All exclusions and color-category rates are outcomes, not a reason to discard generated files.
Reason codes for NA: no_beetle,multiple_animals,nonadult_or_wrong_morphology,non_dorsal,occlusion,truncated,open_wings,too_small,blur,ambiguous_elytra,opposite_polarity,uncertain_patches. Multiple codes permitted.
Use two independent blinded scorers for a credible annotation result, reporting exact count agreement, mean absolute disagreement, and agreement on scorability. Resolve differences by a third blinded review or record both counts. An AI agent can supply exploratory visible-image annotations with a saved audit trail, but these are not expert biological ground truth; a second agent is not an independent biological expert. If independent human scoring is unavailable, deliver the blinded package and label any completed AI scores exploratory rather than silently claiming human validation.

SCORING AND STATISTICS
Primary estimand: mean within-image-seed change in visible dark-patch count for selected feature0x versus original, in C. septempunctata under this fixed generator/prompt/configuration. No directional effect is assumed in advance. This assesses rendered appearance, not the organism's genotype, developmental mechanism or true anatomical spot number.
Pair by generator seed. For each contrast report all32seeds, per-condition scorable counts and reason-code frequencies, number of pairs in which BOTH images are scorable, every observed paired difference, mean and median difference. Complete-pair estimates condition on visibility, which the intervention may alter; report that limitation explicitly. Do not impute NA as zero or remove difficult seeds from all logs. Report paired changes in scorability, pose class and body-size category as separate outcomes. Fewer than16valid paired counts triggers 'insufficient scorable pairs for the planned count analysis', with descriptive results only; it does not trigger seed replacement.
Use a paired nonparametric bootstrap over generator seeds(20,000 resamples, fixed bootstrap seed) for95%confidence intervals, preserving pairing and reporting the effective scorable-pair count. Calculate the count contrast on eligible complete pairs and bootstrap all32paired binary scorability records separately. Save the resampling settings. These intervals describe uncertainty over generation seeds for fixed models and settings; they do not establish biological-population uncertainty or SAE-training uncertainty.
Secondary contrasts:0.5x-original,2x-original,reconstruction-original,other-feature0x-original,random-original. Direct specificity check: selected0x-random on their jointly scorable pairs; report selected0x-other0x as well. An effect merely matching random controls is not feature-specific. Report effect sizes and intervals for all prespecified contrasts, not just those crossing zero. If significance tests are added, distinguish the single primary contrast from exploratory comparisons and apply a declared multiplicity correction to any family of inferential claims.
Repeat the same analysis separately in each replication species and separately in each SAE run; do not pool three species as96independent biological specimens. Consistent direction is a useful replication criterion, but species-specific effects are possible and should be reported, not rewritten as success.
Crop-based BioCLIP image/text cosine is a SECONDARY internal species-alignment proxy because BioCLIP also conditions generation. Score full image and animal crop separately if useful; do not equate either with spot count or independent trait validation. If foreground masks are available and independently checked, a background-only image can test whether alignment remains without the animal, but masking changes image statistics and must be reported. Pose/body-scale/occlusion changes remain potential confounds even after cropping.

BOUNDED POTOO FOLLOW-UP
Only if the matched counterpart of original1969 passes stability: start with N.griseus,32pairedseeds, the same seven conditions and frozen configuration. A neutral pose-control prompt may request 'single perched bird, close-up, whole head visible, sharp focus', but may not request large eyes, open eyes or owl-like traits. Keep this block explicitly exploratory because no prior eye effect was established. N.jamaicensis is a predefined replication after the initial evidence, not a species chosen after looking at favorable images.
Proposed primary exploratory visible endpoint: whether at least one anatomically plausible eye has a clearly discernible iris/pupil boundary. Record0only when the relevant head/eye region is fully visible and sufficiently resolved but the eye is visibly closed/slit-like; recordNA when head is hidden, facing away, cropped, too small, blurred or anatomically ambiguous. Eye visibility is a generated appearance/pose variable, not proof of an evolved morphological trait. Record head pose, number of visible eyes, native head width, and open/slit/closed/uncertain state. Do not label tiny eyelid anatomy from512pixel generation.
If quantitative eye size is explored, prespecify visible iris diameter divided by visible head width, same-view eligibility, and manual landmarks; otherwise do not introduce it after inspecting outputs. Use blinded single-image scoring and paired risk differences with seed-bootstrap intervals. Check controls and pose shifts before attributing visibility changes to the feature. A change dominated by head orientation, zoom or background is not a clean eye-trait effect.

DELIVERABLES AND STOP RULES
Keep source hashes and originals unchanged. Write protocol, condition mapping, blinded package, annotation template/results, per-seed metrics, crop coordinates, image manifest/checksums, figures and a report of both positive and null outcomes to a new experiment directory.
If hardware reports out-of-memory or another hardware failure, stop the active workload, preserve partial logs/checkpoints and report the failure; do not automatically retry smaller batches or silently change the experiment. The user explicitly requested a pause on hardware problems.
The trait branch is complete only to the level actually executed: a failed stability gate is a useful result and stops that branch; a generated annotation package is not completed annotation; AI scoring is not expert scoring; a stable image effect is not yet a validated biological mechanism.
